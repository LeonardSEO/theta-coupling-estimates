Initial public release of Leonard van Hemert's *Sharp logarithmic coupling estimates for theta equality frames in a signed Weil-form realization*.

We prove the exact fixed-frame correction order `beta (log(1/beta))^(4m+5/2)` with constructive constants and a full small-parameter regime. The manuscript includes half-line charge cancellation, matching whole-lattice theta lower bounds, low-energy spectral-mass upper control, the complete signed Schur reduction and the exact change of gamma metric after equality-space elimination.
